      TOURNAMENT RULES

      THE ANTI-BOT CHECK

      • While you sail ranked, the game records a small video of your mouth and jaw only, never your eyes or full face, plus your head's movement as numbers. That's how bots are kept away from the prize pool.
      • It stays on this device and is thrown away, unless the voyage becomes your best of the week in the top 10. Then it's uploaded to private storage.
      • Only the tournament owner can watch it, next to the replay of the same voyage, to check a real person was playing.
      • Every clip of a week is deleted as soon as that week is paid out.
      • A top run without its clip can't win a prize. Practice voyages record nothing at all.

      YOUR FACE IS THE CONTROLLER

      • Lean to steer, tilt your head to aim, open and close your mouth to fire.
      • The camera is read on your device. In practice voyages nothing from it ever leaves your browser, and the video is never shown.
      • Ranked voyages also keep a mouth-and-jaw clip for the anti-bot check. You'll be asked about that separately.
      • The camera switches off when you go back to port.
